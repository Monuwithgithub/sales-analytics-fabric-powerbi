# 📊 Power BI Report

The curated Fabric data is used to build an interactive Sales Analytics dashboard.

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Revenue (USD)
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Revenue per Order
- Average Quantity per Order

## Visualizations

- Monthly Revenue & Orders Trend
- Orders by Product Category
- Revenue by Region
- Orders by Region
- Revenue by Product Category
- Quantity Sold by Product Category
- Top 10 Customers by Revenue

## Filters

- Sales Date
- Sales Month
- Sales Quarter
- Sales Year

## Dashboard Customization

The dashboard was customized with a personal visual theme and consistent colors to improve readability and presentation.
